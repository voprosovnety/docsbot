# Northwind Supplies — Employee Handbook (extract)

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00–16:00 local time. Outside core hours you choose your own
schedule, as long as you work 37.5 hours per week and your team knows when to
expect you.

Anyone contracted for 30 hours or more may compress their week into four days
with a manager's approval.

## Remote work

All roles are remote-first. The London office is open Tuesday to Thursday for
anyone who wants a desk; no booking needed unless you need the meeting room.

Team-wide onsite days happen twice a year. Travel and accommodation are covered
for anyone living more than 50 miles from the office.

## Annual leave

Full-time staff get **28 days** of paid leave per year, plus public holidays.
Leave accrues monthly and up to **5 unused days** may be carried into the next
year. Anything above 5 days is forfeited on 31 December.

Requests for more than 10 consecutive days need two weeks' notice.

## Equipment

New starters choose a laptop from the standard list on day one. The equipment
budget is **£1,800** for the first year and **£600** annually thereafter for
peripherals, chairs, and desks.

Broken equipment is replaced, not deducted from the budget.

## Expenses

Submit expenses through the finance portal by the 25th of the month for
reimbursement in that month's payroll. Anything submitted after the 25th is
paid the following month.

Receipts are required for anything over £20. Client meals are capped at £45 per
person.

## Notice period

Probation is three months, with a one-week notice period on either side. After
probation the notice period is **one month** for individual contributors and
**two months** for managers.
