# Northwind Supplies — Refund and Returns Policy

Effective 1 March 2026. Applies to all orders placed through the online store.

## Return window

Unused items in original packaging may be returned within **30 days** of
delivery. Items bought during a clearance sale may be returned within **14
days**. Custom-cut materials cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged.

## How to start a return

Email returns@northwind-supplies.example with your order number and a photo of
the item. We reply with a prepaid shipping label within one business day.

Do not ship anything back before you receive the label — parcels without a
label cannot be matched to an order and are held for 60 days before disposal.

## Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method within **5 business days** of
the returned item arriving at our warehouse. Bank processing adds a further 2–5
days depending on the card issuer.

Store credit, if you choose it instead, is issued immediately on arrival and
never expires.

## Who pays for shipping

| Reason for return | Return shipping paid by |
|---|---|
| Item arrived damaged or incorrect | Northwind Supplies |
| Item is faulty within the warranty period | Northwind Supplies |
| Changed your mind | Customer (£4.95 deducted from the refund) |
| Wrong size ordered | Customer (£4.95 deducted from the refund) |

## Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund and
place a new order — this is faster than an exchange and keeps your place in
stock allocation.

## Damaged deliveries

Report damage within **48 hours** of delivery with photographs of both the item
and the outer packaging. Claims made after 48 hours can only be processed as
standard returns.
